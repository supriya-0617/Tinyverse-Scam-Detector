# 14 — Technical Risk Register

## 1. Risk Rating Methodology

Every technical, machine learning, privacy, and operational risk is rated across two dimensions:
- **Likelihood (`L`):** `Low (1)`, `Medium (2)`, `High (3)`
- **Impact (`I`):** `Low (1)`, `Medium (2)`, `High (3)`
- **Severity Score (`S = L × I`):**
  - `1–2`: **LOW** (Monitor)
  - `3–4`: **MEDIUM** (Active mitigation required)
  - `6–9`: **HIGH / CRITICAL** (Mandatory architectural mitigation and automated test verification)
